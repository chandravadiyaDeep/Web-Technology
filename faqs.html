General Questions

1. What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is an online college portal that provides academic information, notices, events, and student services.

2. How do I register?

Open the Register page, fill in your details, and submit the form.

3. Can I register for events online?

Yes. Students can register for college events through the Events page.

4. Who can access StudentHub?

Students, faculty members, and administrators can access StudentHub.

5. How do I contact support?

Visit the Contact page or email the college support team.
